Dearest Chris,

After much deliberation behind closed doors, we have reached a decision.

The Faithfuls are gathering. The castle awaits. And a wedding is on the horizon.

But every great mission requires a trusted accomplice.

We have watched you closely. We have assessed your loyalty, your discretion, and your ability to keep a secret. And we believe you have what it takes to join our ranks.

We would like to recruit you as a fellow Traitor.

Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to stand alongside us on our wedding day and officiate our marriage.

You will be entrusted with a secret of the highest importance: the union of two Traitors, in front of an unsuspecting congregation of Faithfuls.

Your duties will include guiding the ceremony, witnessing our vows, and declaring us officially husband and wife.

The Faithfuls must remain none the wiser until the very last moment.

There will be no banishments, no murders, and absolutely no turning on one another at the Round Table. (At least not until after the open bar.)

So, Chris, we ask you:

Will you join our team of Traitors and officiate our wedding?

We trust you will make the right decision.

After all, once a Traitor, always a Traitor.

With love and utmost secrecy,

Dasha & Matt
